Audiobook Percentage Calculator

What Does the Audiobook Percentage Calculator Do?

The Audiobook Percentage Calculator calculates how much of an audiobook has been completed as a single clean percentage. It divides your listened time by total audiobook length so you can track reading goals, update StoryGraph / Goodreads, or share progress.

Completion Percentage (%) = (Listened Minutes ÷ Total Minutes) × 100
