Audiobook Price & Narrator Cost Estimator

Audiobook costs vary whether you are a listener deciding between a monthly credit versus retail purchase, or an indie author estimating studio budget under ACX's Per Finished Hour (PFH) model. This calculator simplifies the math by generating cost-per-hour benchmarks.

Audiobook Pricing Formulas

  • Total Duration (Hours) = Hours + (Minutes ÷ 60)
  • Cost per Hour = Total Price ÷ Total Duration (Hours)
  • Cost per Minute = Cost per Hour ÷ 60
  • Narrator Studio Total = Finished Hours × PFH Rate

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Audiobook production cost is calculated by multiplying finished audiobook runtime (hours) by the narrator's rate Per Finished Hour (PFH), then adding any mastering, editing, or proofing fees.

PFH (Per Finished Hour) is the industry-standard billing unit. It is the amount charged per hour of completed, mastered audio, irrespective of how many raw recording takes were performed.

If an audiobook's cash price is less than the cost of one subscription credit (typically ~$14.95), it is more cost-effective to buy it with cash and reserve your credit for audiobooks with higher retail prices or longer runtimes.
