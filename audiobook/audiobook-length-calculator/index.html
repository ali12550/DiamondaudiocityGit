How to Use the Audiobook Length Calculator

1. Enter your manuscript word count, or choose page count.

3. The calculator instantly generates total listening time in hours, minutes, and exact HH:MM:SS format.

What Does the Audiobook Length Calculator Do?

The Audiobook Length Calculator converts written manuscript content into expected finished audio runtime. By combining word count or page count with realistic narrator pace (words per minute), authors and voice artists can forecast exact recording durations before booking studio time.

Standard Narration Formulas

Audiobook Minutes = Total Words ÷ Narration WPM
Audiobook Hours = (Total Words ÷ Narration WPM) ÷ 60

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Audiobook runtime is calculated by dividing total words by narrator speed (typically 150 WPM). For example, a 90,000-word manuscript at 150 WPM results in approximately 10 finished audio hours (600 minutes).

Professional audiobook narrators read at an average pace of 150 to 160 words per minute. Fiction with dramatic pauses tends toward 145-150 WPM, while business and self-help non-fiction is typically 155-165 WPM.
