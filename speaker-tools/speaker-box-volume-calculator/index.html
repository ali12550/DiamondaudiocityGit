How This Calculator Works
  • Uses Thiele/Small loudspeaker parameters
  • Sealed boxes use Qtc alignment (default 0.707)
  • Ported boxes use Butterworth approximation
  • Internal volume excludes wood thickness & bracing
Formulas & Notes
  • Sealed Box Volume: Vb_box = Vb × 1.0–1.5 (depends on desired Q)
  • Ported Box Volume: Vb_box = Vb × 1.0–1.25 (adjusted for tuning frequency)
  • Adjust box volume to balance low-frequency extension vs. transient response.
  • Ported boxes require correct tuning frequency and port size for desired bass performance.

How to Use

  • Enter the Thiele-Small Vb value (manufacturer spec for your driver).
  • Select the enclosure type: Sealed or Ported.
  • If ported, enter the desired tuning frequency and number of ports.
  • The tool calculates the optimal box volume in liters or cubic feet.
  • Use the results to design or build your speaker enclosure.

FAQs

Vb is the speaker’s recommended box volume for optimal performance based on its parameters (from manufacturer specs).

Sealed boxes typically require slightly larger volumes than ported boxes. Ported boxes allow tuning via the port but need accurate calculations for optimal performance.

This tool works for speakers with known Thiele-Small parameters. Unknown parameters may lead to inaccurate results.
