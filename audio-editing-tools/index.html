Audio Editing Tools

Free online audio editing tools. Trim, merge, boost, speed up, normalize, reverse, and loop your audio files directly in your browser.

Audio Trimmer

Cut and trim audio files. Set start and end points to extract the perfect clip.

Audio Merger

Combine multiple audio files into one. Merge songs, podcasts, or voice recordings.

Volume Booster

Increase or decrease audio volume. Amplify quiet recordings or reduce loud ones.

Speed Changer

Change audio playback speed without affecting pitch. Speed up or slow down.

Audio Normalizer

Normalize audio levels for consistent volume across your files.

Audio Reverser

Reverse audio files for creative effects or to discover hidden messages.

Loop Generator

Create seamless audio loops for music production and background tracks.

Browser-Based Audio Editing

All our audio editing tools work directly in your browser. No software installation needed, and your files stay private - they're processed locally on your device.

Supported Formats

  • Input: MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, FLAC, WebM
  • Output: MP3, WAV (depending on tool)
  • Max File Size: Varies by browser memory
