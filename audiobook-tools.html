Playback Speed Guide

  • 1x Speed: Best for complex non-fiction, new narrators
  • 1.25x Speed: Good starting point for faster listening
  • 1.5x Speed: Popular choice for experienced listeners
  • 2x Speed: For familiar content or re-listens
