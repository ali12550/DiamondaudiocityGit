Frequently Asked Questions

For a reliable reading, tap at least 8-16 times. The more taps you make, the more accurate the average BPM will be. Professional DJs often tap for 16-32 beats to ensure accuracy.

Tap along to the main beat of the song - usually the kick drum or bass. In most music, this is the steady pulse you would naturally nod your head or tap your foot to. Avoid tapping to hi-hats or other faster rhythmic elements.
