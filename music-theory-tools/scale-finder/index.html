A Major Scale

Scale Information
Intervals:

W - W - H - W - W - W - H

Formula:

1 - 2 - 3 - 4 - 5 - 6 - 7

Mood/Character:

Bright, happy, uplifting

Common Uses:

Pop, rock, classical, country

Scale Reference

Major scales have a bright, happy sound due to their major 3rd interval. They're used in uplifting music.

Minor scales have a darker, more melancholic sound. The natural minor has a flat 3rd, 6th, and 7th.

Pentatonic scales have 5 notes instead of 7. They remove the "avoid" notes, making them great for improvisation. The minor pentatonic is the foundation of blues and rock guitar solos.
