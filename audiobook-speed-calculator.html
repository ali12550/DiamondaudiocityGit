Tips for Choosing the Right Speed

Speeds between 1.1x–1.4x preserve natural narration pacing while reducing total time. For efficiency, 1.5x–1.75x balances comprehension and time savings. Speeds above 2x are suited for advanced listeners or re-listening sessions. Consider narrator rhythm, content complexity, and personal pace before selecting your final playback rate.
