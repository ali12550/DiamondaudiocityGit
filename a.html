About Zobel Networks

A Zobel network is an RC (resistor-capacitor) circuit connected in parallel with a speaker driver or load. It flattens the impedance curve at higher frequencies, ensuring amplifier stability and precise crossover performance. Proper Zobel network design prevents audio distortion, reduces inductive peaks, and optimizes high-frequency response for tweeters, midrange drivers, and subwoofers.

  • Impedance flattening and reactive load management
  • High-frequency stabilization for audio fidelity
  • Optimal crossover accuracy and phase alignment
  • Amplifier protection and distortion prevention
  • Speaker RC network design for subwoofers and tweeters
