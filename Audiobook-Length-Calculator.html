What Is an Audiobook Length Calculator?

An audiobook length calculator estimates total audiobook duration by dividing the manuscript word count by the narrator's words per minute rate (WPM). It factors in reading speed, narration style, language selection, pauses & breaks, and converts written content into listening time in minutes, hours, or HH:MM:SS format. Ideal for authors, narrators, producers, and audiobook listeners who want accurate duration estimates.

Formula Used to Calculate Audiobook Length

This calculator uses standard narration math based on words per minute and average word count per page. Adjust for narration style and language selection for more precise total duration. 

Listening time (minutes) = Word Count ÷ Narrator WPM
Listening time (hours)   = (Word Count ÷ WPM) ÷ 60
Example:
90,000 words ÷ 150 WPM = 600 minutes → 10 hours

Supports chapter-based estimation and pages-to-time conversion when exact word count is unavailable.
